HOW TO USE THIS MAP

  • Pick a need from the tile rail — AIR, STRUCTURE, CONTROLS, TOWING, PARKING, MECHANIC.
  • Tap any badge, then hit NAVIGATE NOW. Coordinates are handed to the Google Maps app on your phone.
  • Swipe the bottom panel up for details. Swipe it down to get the map back.
  • The map is locked to Greater Kolkata, so you can never pan off into nowhere.

ABOUT THE DATA — READ THIS

This map mixes two kinds of record, and the app tells you which is which.

VERIFIED LISTINGS — authorised Hero, Honda, TVS, Bajaj and Royal Enfield service centres. These carry a real, publicly published phone number, so the CALL button is live and the detail panel shows where the listing came from. Their opening hours are not confirmed, so the map shows a yellow dot and CALL AHEAD rather than guessing. Ring before you tow.

SEED RECORDS — the rest of the map. The neighbourhood and coordinates are real Kolkata geography, but the business names, hours and phone numbers are synthetic placeholders. CALL is disabled on these, because dialling a placeholder could reach a stranger.

To promote a seed record, open assets/js/data.js, put in a real consented number and set contact: true. The call button activates on its own.

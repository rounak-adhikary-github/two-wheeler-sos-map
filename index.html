HOW TO USE THIS MAP

  • Pick a need from the tile rail — MECHANIC, TOWING, TYRE/AIR, BATTERY, STRUCTURE, CONTROLS — or pick your bike's brand.
  • Tap any badge, then hit NAVIGATE NOW. Coordinates are handed to the Google Maps app on your phone.
  • To get back out of a place: tap the red ✕ in the panel bar, or just tap anywhere on the map. Both return you to the full list.
  • Swipe the bottom panel up for full details, down to get the map back. Tapping the bar cycles it too.
  • The map is locked to Greater Kolkata, so you can never pan off into nowhere.

ABOUT THE DATA — READ THIS

Every listing on this map is a real business. There are no fabricated records.

AUTHORISED SERVICE CENTRES — Hero, Honda, TVS, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Yamaha, Suzuki and KTM, from manufacturer directories and brand dealer locators.

LOCAL WORKSHOPS — independent neighbourhood garages. Usually cheaper and faster for a puncture or a roadside fix than a main dealer.

TYRE / AIR — puncture and tyre shops. BATTERY — Exide and Amaron dealers, for when the bike will not crank. CONTROLS / STRUCTURE — spare-parts shops. TOWING — 24x7 recovery operators.

Phone numbers are printed exactly as the source published them, so CALL is live on almost every record, and the detail panel shows which directory each number came from. Listings were captured from manufacturer directories, public business directories and Google Maps place listings.

A green dot means open 24 hours, where the source confirmed it. For everything else the opening time is not published, so the app does not guess — you get a yellow dot and CALL AHEAD. Ring before you tow your bike anywhere.
